Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data GuardBot and related services ("the Service"), provided by Omar / OMAR.DEV, collect, how it is used, and your choices. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. What Data Is Collected

To provide moderation and security features, the Service may process:

  • Discord IDs — server, channel, user, and role IDs, needed to identify where actions happen and who they apply to.
  • Message content — read only to evaluate it against auto-moderation rules (spam detection, scam links, mass mentions, etc.). Flagged messages may be briefly logged to your server's own mod-log channel, which is visible only to your staff.
  • Server configuration — settings you choose, such as which channel logs go to or which role is used for verification, stored so the bot behaves consistently.
  • Moderation records — warnings, timeouts, and similar actions taken through the bot, stored so repeat behavior can be tracked and escalated appropriately.

2. What Is Not Collected

  • The bot does not read or store messages from channels it cannot see, or from servers it is not added to.
  • The bot does not collect payment details, email addresses, or personal identity information beyond what Discord itself exposes through its API (e.g. a user's Discord ID and username).

3. AI-Assisted Moderation

Some packages include an optional AI "smart mode" that sends borderline message text to a third-party AI provider for classification (e.g. to judge whether a message is a scam or harassment) when simpler automated checks are inconclusive. Only the message text necessary for that evaluation is sent, and it is used solely to return a moderation judgment — not stored by me beyond normal logging described above. This feature can be turned on or off per server.

4. How Data Is Stored

Configuration and moderation records are stored in a local database tied to the specific server the bot is running for. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. Data may be visible to me (the developer) for the purpose of debugging or providing support during the after-sale support period.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Removing the bot from your server stops any further data collection. To request deletion of stored moderation records or configuration data for your server, contact me using the details below.

6. Third Parties

The Service communicates with Discord's API to function, which is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy. If AI smart mode is enabled, flagged message text may also be sent to the relevant AI provider in use at the time, subject to that provider's own privacy terms.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use on Discord servers in accordance with Discord's own age requirements and is not directed at collecting data from children beyond what Discord itself permits on its platform.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent via email or WhatsApp.
